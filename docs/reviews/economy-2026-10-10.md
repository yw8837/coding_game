## Health summary: CONCERNS

No mandatory purchase gate, failure charge, hint penalty, repeat payout loop, or credit shortage blocking the authored route was found in the normal source action paths. Rewards and prices permit access to useful tools near the start of career play. A concrete purchase promise mismatch exists: the 600 cr second monitor only adds its comparison panel in the full configuration editor at the baseline. It does not add that panel in the default guided editor or JavaScript editor, and the baseline CSS hides even that panel in containers at or below 820 px. The main task has extended the panel to all three editing paths and retained it in a stacked narrow layout; this reviewer confirmed those source changes, while runtime validation belongs to the main task.

Credit spending finishes early compared with the remaining reward supply. That is a pacing observation, not evidence that the game needs harsher costs or grinding. The game has finite content, no recurring credit obligations, and a personal project as its final objective. Enjoyment, learning transfer, completion times, and whether the final release feels rewarding require player observation.

| Cafe + all career + all practice/bonuses/lab/audits | **9,775** | **3,743** | Absolute authored gross total; all purchases leave **7,665 cr** |

Required career earnings are `4 × (200 + 350 + 550) = 4,400 cr / 1,640 XP`. All career earnings are `6 × (200 + 350 + 550) = 6,600 cr / 2,460 XP`. Adding optional content to a 12-job route gives 7,575 cr / 2,923 XP; choosing any of the six extra jobs adds its district reward. These are content-based ranges, not time estimates.

No XP-based power curve is present: XP records completion, while gates use cafe OPEN and completed career counts. The reward steps 200 → 350 → 550 do not confer power or access themselves. Each district permits six candidates immediately, and `careerNext` prioritizes a district still below four completions. Skipped jobs remain available after unlocks and after the ending. There is no credit-induced progression dead zone in these rules.

## Replay and degenerate strategies

- Cafe deployments return early if the mission is already completed (`engine.ts:138`). OPEN only changes its flag and badge once; it awards no credits.
- Career delivery returns early for a completed job (`career.ts:46`), and requires inspection, a fresh passed proof, successful evaluation, and a valid reply. Reply styles and configuration/JavaScript solution modes share the same fixed reward. The saved completion IDs and upgrade IDs must be unique.
- Practice only pays if a proof passed and its ID is absent from `practice.completed` (`engine.ts:86–88`). Draft edits discard proof, not completion history.
- Bonuses consult their claimed-ID list before payment. A new color selection cannot reset the claimed color bonus.
- Lab UI and terminal tests converge on `recordLabProof` (`code-lab-terminal.ts:92–95`). Payment depends on the completed-ID list, not proof existence, seed, minimum operation count, or reopening an editor. Deleting/restoring files or changing test data does not delete the first-completion record.
- Audits pay at most once per completed lab job (`code-lab-state.ts:28–30`). Finding more counterexamples remains practice without further money.
- Release/ending replay does not pay, and the ending captures a fixed reply basis so later optional jobs do not silently change it.
- No failure/hint charge appears in these transaction branches. The example-code function in the optional lab helps reach a first test success but is finite, openly disclosed, and does not provide an infinite resource loop. Learning from that help is a human-observation question.

These conclusions describe normal engine actions and valid saves. There is no claim that editable local offline saves are a secure anti-cheat ledger. Resetting the entire save creates a new finite playthrough rather than persistent accumulated metacurrency.
